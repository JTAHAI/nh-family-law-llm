## Short description

Local-first New Hampshire family-law research and record-organization workbench with source checks and human-review safeguards.

## Description

New Hampshire Family Law LLM is a local-first workbench for organizing family-law records, exploring legal-information workflows, and reviewing source-grounded research material. It is designed for people who want to understand the workspace before they add personal information: the app includes a clearly labeled fictional New Hampshire sample corpus made only from synthetic orientation material.

Create separate local matter workspaces, reopen them later, add records without replacing originals, and switch between saved corpora from one installation. The app can help you inventory documents, review source cards, inspect citations and exact-source material, create working drafts, and prepare review packets.

Your personal records are not treated as legal authority. Official-source research, private matter records, citation and quote checks, privacy review, and human review remain separate safeguards. The app is not a lawyer, does not provide legal advice, and does not make a document filing-ready.

Use the fictional sample first, then decide whether the local workflow is right for your own records.

## Features

- Explore a fictional New Hampshire sample corpus before adding personal information.
- Create, reopen, and switch between local family-matter workspaces.
- Keep original records read-only while tracking inventory and follow-on imports.
- Review source cards, citations, quote checks, and current-source status.
- Produce review-required working drafts and evidence or review packages.
- Use local-first privacy, record-separation, and human-review safeguards.
