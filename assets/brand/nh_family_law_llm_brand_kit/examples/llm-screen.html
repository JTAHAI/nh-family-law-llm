Checklist: what a parent/contact schedule should cover

A useful parenting/contact schedule should be specific enough that both parents know regular time, exchanges, holidays, transportation, communication, and safety restrictions if any. The legal analysis still ties back to parental rights and responsibilities and the child’s best interest.

  • Regular weekly schedule and exchange location.
  • Holiday and school-break schedule.
  • Transportation responsibilities and backup plan.
  • Communication method and emergency exceptions.
