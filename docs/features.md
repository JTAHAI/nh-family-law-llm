    <article><h2>Read scans and audio</h2><p>Create searchable OCR derivatives and transcribe supported audio with packaged offline engines. Check the output against the original.</p></article>
    <article><h2>Review drafts and exports</h2><p>Work from cited sources and selected evidence. Inspect tracked changes, privacy findings, and review status before exporting.</p></article>
