# Independent NH authority review packet

This packet is a handoff, not a completed review. A qualified New Hampshire reviewer should receive the exact manifest digest, preserved original-byte snapshots, extraction records, source-to-span mappings, currentness/update report, session-law/effective-date records, enabled workflow matrix, calculator/form materials, and adversarial scenario results for the candidate release.

Required decisions: source identity; original/extraction integrity; effective intervals; amendment and repeal treatment; proposition wording, exceptions, and scope; holdings and later-treatment limits; form/currentness; calculator table vintage; and whether each workflow may be enabled. Record each decision with scope, date, reviewer authorization, findings, limitations, and the reviewed digest. Reviewer contact information belongs outside the repository.
