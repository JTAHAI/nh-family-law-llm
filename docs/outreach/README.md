# Outreach materials

These files prepare manual outreach. No emails have been sent by this repository, and nothing in this folder counts as attorney review, pilot evidence, legal signoff, or GA evidence. Template-only outreach material does not count as attorney review.

Use these materials after local checks pass and after a human chooses specific recipients. Outreach should be sent one message at a time through Microsoft 365/Outlook or another approved mailbox, with real correspondence saved outside the source repo unless a redacted evidence artifact is intentionally added later.

Possible outreach channels:

- New Hampshire Law / University of New Hampshire School of Law clinics, faculty, or legal innovation contacts.
- New Hampshire attorneys.
- New Hampshire legal aid and access-to-justice organizations.
- UNew Hampshire Farmington public-service, pre-law, or student-usability contacts.
- UNE Biddeford pre-law, legal-studies, or student-usability contacts.

UMF and UNE usability channels do not replace licensed New Hampshire attorney review unless a licensed New Hampshire attorney actually reviews the work and provides evidence.
