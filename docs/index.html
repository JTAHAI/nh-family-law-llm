What it supports

A research and organization workbench—not an automated law office.

Local matter organization

Create and switch between separate local case corpora, reopen a matter, add records later, and preserve a clear record trail.

Source-grounded research

Inspect source cards, citations, dates, and exact supporting material. If current authority is unavailable or stale, the workflow is designed to say so.

Review-required drafting

Produce working drafts and review packets with visible blockers. No output is a filing-ready document merely because it was generated.

Privacy-aware processing

Keep originals intact while reviewing duplicate records, OCR derivatives, redaction candidates, and local export receipts.

Boundaries matter

Designed to fail closed when the evidence is not there.

  • It is not legal advice and does not replace counsel or the court.
  • Private records are not legal authority.
  • Current-law claims require current, admitted official sources.
  • Human review, source checking, privacy review, and filing decisions remain separate.
