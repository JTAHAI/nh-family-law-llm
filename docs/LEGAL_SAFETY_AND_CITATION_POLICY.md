
# Legal safety and citation policy

1. A result must identify jurisdiction, authority type, citation, status, source, and pinpoint.
2. Current law outranks historical material. A source with unknown effective date cannot support a definitive present-law statement.
3. Statutory text and session laws must be reconciled after amendments. Case summaries must be checked against the official opinion and later treatment.
4. Forms and self-help pages are procedural aids, not substitutes for governing statutes and rules.
5. Child-support outputs are audit worksheets only unless every required input, applicable guideline, deviation, effective date, and order term has been verified.
6. Generated pleadings require human review. Do not file invented facts, unverified quotations, or confidential information.
7. Existing support and parenting orders remain in force unless modified, vacated, or stayed by an authorized tribunal.
