
# Parenting-time modification preparation — generalized New Hampshire checklist

This is a neutral preparation tool, not a prediction of outcome.

- Obtain the complete signed parenting order, parenting plan, later modifications, and docket history.
- Identify the statutory modification route and required findings under current RSA 461-A; do not rely on informal schedule changes alone.
- Build a child-centered proposed plan covering school nights, exchanges, transportation, holidays, vacations, activities, medical care, communications, weather, and dispute resolution.
- Document stable housing, work schedule, transportation, school/daycare logistics, historical caregiving, attendance at appointments and activities, and ability to support the children's individual needs.
- Preserve complete communications in context. Use a factual chronology; do not manufacture conflict or disparage the other parent.
- Identify witnesses and admissible records. Resolve hearsay, authentication, confidentiality, and subpoena issues before hearing.
- Continue following the current order unless lawful emergency relief or a modification is entered.
- Analyze child support separately. More residential time does not itself rewrite the Uniform Support Order.
