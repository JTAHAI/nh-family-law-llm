
# Manchester-area self-help and lower-cost legal assistance

Start with official, current intake pages because eligibility, clinic schedules, court location, and program scope change.

- New Hampshire Judicial Branch Self-Help Center and Family Division forms.
- 603 Legal Aid statewide intake and legal-information/referral services.
- New Hampshire Legal Assistance, subject to matter and financial eligibility.
- New Hampshire Bar Association lawyer-referral, reduced-fee/modest-means, and limited-scope resources where currently offered.
- Limited-scope representation: confirm the current court rule, written engagement, and precisely which tasks counsel will handle.
- Fee-waiver and mediation-fee materials: use the current official court form and instructions.

For a post-divorce parenting or support matter, confirm the correct Family Division location from the existing docket/order before filing. Obtain the current forms and filing/service instructions directly from the Judicial Branch; an old packet may have obsolete deadlines or required fields.

Seek prompt legal review where there are abuse allegations, interstate jurisdiction, relocation, contempt/jail exposure, complex or self-employment income, hidden assets, a GAL dispute, child-protection involvement, or an approaching appeal/reconsideration deadline.
