
# Child-support order audit — generalized New Hampshire checklist

1. Obtain the signed Uniform Support Order, guideline worksheet, financial affidavits, incorporated agreement, and every later order.
2. Record the order date, number of children, actual residential schedule, each parent's income findings, health-insurance cost, work-related childcare, other allowed adjustments, arrears, and income-withholding terms.
3. Determine whether NH DHHS/BCSS is enforcing an existing court order or whether an administrative establishment process under RSA 161-C is involved. Read the notice and appeal/review language; do not assume another state's procedure applies.
4. Recalculate only from verified current inputs and the guideline effective on the legally relevant date. Ordinary rent, car payments, and household expenses are not automatically deductible merely because they are real burdens.
5. Test separately for the applicable modification route, effective-date/retroactivity rules, and statutory deviation findings.
6. Do not reduce, suspend, or redirect payment without an entered order or other lawful authorization.
