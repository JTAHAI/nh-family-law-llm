
# Maine-to-New Hampshire jurisdiction crosswalk

This document is a migration map, not a source of substantive law. It permits architectural reuse without carrying Maine authority into the active New Hampshire corpus.

| Generic concept | Maine-oriented implementation term | New Hampshire implementation term / source family |
|---|---|---|
| Custody | Parental rights and responsibilities terminology varies | Parental rights and responsibilities; decision-making and residential responsibility — RSA 461-A |
| Parenting schedule | Contact / residence provisions | Parenting plan and allocation of residential responsibility — RSA 461-A |
| Child-support guideline | Maine Title 19-A guideline corpus | RSA 458-C plus current worksheets, Uniform Support Order, rules, and session laws |
| Support enforcement agency | Maine DHHS structure | NH DHHS Bureau of Child Support Services; RSA 161-B and RSA 161-C; distinguish administration from court authority |
| Interstate custody | Maine UCCJEA implementation | New Hampshire UCCJEA — RSA 458-A |
| Interstate support | Maine UIFSA implementation | New Hampshire UIFSA — verify current codification, including RSA 546-B and cross-references |
| Divorce court | Maine District Court family matters | New Hampshire Circuit Court, Family Division, subject to statute and current rules |
| Highest state precedent | Maine Supreme Judicial Court / New Hampshire Supreme Court | New Hampshire Supreme Court |

No Maine statute, rule, form, agency manual, or opinion should be indexed as New Hampshire authority. Shared federal authority must be separately tagged and its jurisdictional role stated.
