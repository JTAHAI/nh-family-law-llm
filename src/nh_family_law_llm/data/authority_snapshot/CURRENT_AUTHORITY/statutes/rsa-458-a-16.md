## Source-grounded extract (73 words)

Before a child-custody determination is made, notice and an opportunity to be heard under RSA 458-A:7 must be given to all persons entitled to notice under New Hampshire law, any parent whose parental rights have not been terminated, and any person with physical custody. The chapter does not determine enforceability of a determination made without notice or an opportunity to be heard, and joinder and intervention are governed by New Hampshire law.

## Structured propositions

- Jurisdiction and due-process notice are separate requirements.
- Persons with physical custody and parents whose rights remain intact may require notice even if they are outside New Hampshire.
