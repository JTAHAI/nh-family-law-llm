## Source-grounded extract (96 words)

The total support obligation shall be determined by multiplying the parents' total net income by the appropriate statutory percentage. The total child support obligation is divided between the parents in proportion to their respective incomes, subject to the child-care and medical-support rules. If the obligor's gross income is below the self-support reserve and the court finds no voluntary unemployment or underemployment, the court shall order the minimum support order. The presumptive reasonable medical-support obligation is 4 percent of each parent's individual gross income unless written or on-record findings establish a different amount under RSA 458-C:5.

## Structured propositions

- The statute supplies a percentage table keyed to combined net income and number of children; DHHS publishes interpolated schedules.
- The parents’ proportional shares are based on their respective adjusted incomes.
- A self-support reserve can limit the presumptive obligation, but it does not create a general deduction for ordinary living expenses.
- Medical support is a separate required finding and presumptively equals four percent of individual gross income unless adjusted with findings.
