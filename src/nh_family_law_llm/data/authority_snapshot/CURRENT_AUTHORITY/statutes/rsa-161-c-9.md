## Source-grounded extract (89 words)

A hearings officer conducts the fair hearing under RSA 541-A and must issue a decision within 20 days. The officer considers the child's needs, the parent's income and property, ability to borrow and earn, accrued debt, the parent's needs, responsibility for other dependents, and RSA 458-C. A default decision may be challenged within 15 days for cause. A subsequent legal support order supersedes the administrative decision to the extent they differ. Administrative orders are filed with the superior-court clerk and have the same force and effect as court orders.

## Structured propositions

- Either the responsible parent or commissioner may seek modification based on changed circumstances; prospective modification requires good cause and a material change.
- A later legal order can supersede the administrative decision to the extent of conflict, but the mechanism and forum must be determined from the live case posture.
- The statute gives an RSA 161-C administrative order enforceable force; it should not be treated as an informal agency calculation.
