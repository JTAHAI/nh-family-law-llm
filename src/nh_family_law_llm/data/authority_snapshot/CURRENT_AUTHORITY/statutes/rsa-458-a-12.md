## Source-grounded extract (93 words)

Except for temporary emergency jurisdiction, New Hampshire may make an initial child-custody determination only when it is the child's home state at commencement, or was the home state within the prior six months while the child is absent and a parent remains; when the home state lacks or declines jurisdiction and significant-connection and substantial-evidence requirements are met; when all otherwise qualified courts decline in favor of New Hampshire; or when no other state qualifies. These criteria are the exclusive jurisdictional basis, and physical presence or personal jurisdiction alone is neither necessary nor sufficient.

## Structured propositions

- The home-state question is the first jurisdictional checkpoint.
- Physical presence in New Hampshire does not by itself establish ordinary initial custody jurisdiction.
