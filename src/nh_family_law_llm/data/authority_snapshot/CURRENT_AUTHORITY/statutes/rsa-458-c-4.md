## Source-grounded extract (64 words)

Subject to RSA 458-C:5, the guidelines shall be applied in all child support cases, including temporary orders and orders modifying support. There is a rebuttable presumption that the guideline result is the correct amount. A written finding, or a specific finding on the record, that applying the guidelines would be unjust or inappropriate under RSA 458-C:5 is sufficient to rebut that presumption.

## Structured propositions

- Guidelines apply to original, temporary, and modification orders.
- A non-guideline amount requires findings tied to the statutory special-circumstances criteria.
- An agreement between parents does not eliminate judicial review of whether the guideline result is unjust or inappropriate.
