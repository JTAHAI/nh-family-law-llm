## Source-grounded extract (92 words)

New Hampshire has temporary emergency jurisdiction when the child is present here and has been abandoned or emergency protection is necessary because the child, a sibling, or a parent is subjected to or threatened with mistreatment or abuse. If a prior enforceable determination or qualifying proceeding exists elsewhere, the New Hampshire order must specify time to obtain an order from the state with ordinary jurisdiction and remains effective only until that order is obtained or the period expires. The courts must communicate to resolve the emergency and protect the parties and child.

## Structured propositions

- Emergency jurisdiction is temporary and safety-focused; it does not automatically create permanent modification jurisdiction.
- Communication with the court having ordinary UCCJEA jurisdiction is mandatory in the circumstances stated by the section.
