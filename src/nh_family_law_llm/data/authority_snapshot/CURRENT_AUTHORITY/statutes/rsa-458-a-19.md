## Source-grounded extract (86 words)

Except for emergency jurisdiction, if jurisdiction exists because the person invoking it engaged in unjustifiable conduct, the court shall decline jurisdiction unless all parents and persons acting as parents acquiesce, the otherwise competent state determines New Hampshire is more appropriate, or no other state qualifies. The court may fashion a safety remedy and stay the case until a proceeding begins in the proper state. Necessary and reasonable expenses, including attorney fees and travel and child-care costs, are generally assessed against the invoking party unless clearly inappropriate.

## Structured propositions

- Wrongful forum-creating conduct can defeat jurisdiction and produce fee consequences.
- Safety-preserving temporary remedies remain available even when ordinary jurisdiction is declined.
