## Source-grounded extract (70 words)

The purpose of this chapter is to establish a uniform system to be used in the determination of the amount of child support, to minimize the economic consequences to children, and to comply with applicable federal law. Both parents shall share responsibility for economic support of the children. The children in an obligor's initial family are entitled to a standard of living equal to that of the obligor's subsequent families.

## Structured propositions

- RSA 458-C establishes a uniform guideline system rather than a free-form ability-to-pay test.
- Both parents share responsibility for economic support.
- The guideline percentage varies with income and number of children.
