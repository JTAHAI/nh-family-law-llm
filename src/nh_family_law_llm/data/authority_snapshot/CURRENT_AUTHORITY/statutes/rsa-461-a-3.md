## Source-grounded extract (64 words)

The procedure in parental-rights cases, including child support, is the same as for divorce and legal-separation petitions under RSA 458. Upon proper application and notice, the court may revise and modify its orders and make necessary new orders. When spouses or unmarried parents live apart, either may petition for parental-rights and support orders. Jurisdiction is limited by the UCCJEA when another state has jurisdiction.

## Structured propositions

- A parenting case can include support and can be brought by married or unmarried parents who live apart.
- Modification requires a proper application and notice and remains subject to the more specific statutory gates.
- Interstate facts require a separate UCCJEA jurisdiction analysis.
