## Source-grounded extract (82 words)

At every hearing in which child support is involved, the party seeking the order shall file a child support guideline worksheet, as published by the department of health and human services, division of child support services, duly completed by either the party or attorney. The other party shall file a child support guideline worksheet if the guideline amount is in dispute. The worksheet that results in the ordered child support obligation shall be kept in the file and available to the parties.

## Structured propositions

- A guideline worksheet is mandatory at a support hearing even when the parties agree or request a deviation.
- The resulting worksheet is retained in the court file and should be obtained when auditing an existing order.
- When a party has not disclosed income, the statute permits a reasonable estimate for worksheet completion.
