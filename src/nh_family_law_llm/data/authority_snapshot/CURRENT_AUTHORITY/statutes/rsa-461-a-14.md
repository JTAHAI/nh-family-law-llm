## Source-grounded extract (83 words)

After a divorce, separation, parentage, support, or parental-rights petition—including specified DHHS petitions—the court may order support and education for the children. Support ordinarily continues to age 18, but may continue for a full-time secondary, elementary, charter, or home-education student until graduation or two months after age 19, whichever occurs first; disability rules may extend support subject to statutory limits. No modification may alter arrearages due before the modification motion was filed. Each order must address health coverage and uninsured medical expenses.

## Structured propositions

- When an existing Family Division order controls, DHHS ordinarily administers or enforces it; reduction requires modification by the issuing authority.
- The statute requires wage assignment subject to RSA 458-B exceptions and contains lien, judgment, medical-support, benefit-suspension, and enforcement provisions.
- Termination for one child in a multi-child order is a substantial change and may be pre-calculated as a step-down in a new order.
- Court-ordered support cannot require adult college expenses except through a qualifying agreement under RSA 461-A:21.
