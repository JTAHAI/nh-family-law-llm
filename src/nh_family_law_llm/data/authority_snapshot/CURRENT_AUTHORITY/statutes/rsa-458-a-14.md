## Source-grounded extract (68 words)

Except for temporary emergency jurisdiction, New Hampshire may not modify another state's child-custody determination unless New Hampshire has initial jurisdiction under the home-state or significant-connection provisions and either the other state determines it no longer has exclusive continuing jurisdiction or that New Hampshire is more convenient, or a court determines that the child, parents, and any person acting as a parent no longer reside in the other state.

## Structured propositions

- An interstate parenting modification requires both a New Hampshire jurisdictional basis and loss or relinquishment of the issuing state's continuing jurisdiction.
- The rule prevents competing modifications based solely on one parent’s relocation.
