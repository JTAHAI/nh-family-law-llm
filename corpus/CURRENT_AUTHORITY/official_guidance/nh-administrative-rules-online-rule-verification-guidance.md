## Source-grounded extract (75 words)

The Administrative Rules office cautions researchers to double-check every online rule for later amendments or expiration. An effective rule may not appear online until the agency certifies it after editing, and an online rule may remain stale until certification. The official guidance directs users to the rule's source note and agency filing history and, when needed, to obtain the as-filed official rule from the agency, Administrative Rules office, State Library, or New Hampshire Law Library.
