## Source-grounded extract (73 words)

A New Hampshire court that made a custody determination consistently with the UCCJEA retains exclusive continuing jurisdiction until a New Hampshire court determines that the required significant connection and substantial evidence no longer exist, or a New Hampshire or other-state court determines that the child, parents, and any person acting as a parent no longer reside here. If continuing jurisdiction is lost, New Hampshire may modify only if it again has initial-determination jurisdiction.

## Structured propositions

- The issuing state ordinarily retains control until the statutory connection/residence conditions end.
- A move does not automatically transfer jurisdiction without the necessary judicial determination.
