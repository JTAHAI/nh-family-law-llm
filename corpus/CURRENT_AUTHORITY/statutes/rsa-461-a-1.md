## Source-grounded extract (64 words)

Decision-making responsibility” means responsibility to make decisions for the child. “Parental rights and responsibilities” means all rights and responsibilities parents have concerning their child. A “parenting plan” is a written plan describing each parent's rights and responsibilities. A “parenting schedule” specifies the days and hours when each parent has parenting time. “Residential responsibility” means a parent's responsibility to provide a home for the child.

## Structured propositions

- These are the operative New Hampshire terms and should be preferred over generic custody labels.
- Decision-making and residential responsibility are separate concepts that can be allocated differently.
