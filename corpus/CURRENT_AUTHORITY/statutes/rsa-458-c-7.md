## Source-grounded extract (78 words)

An obligor or obligee may apply to whichever tribunal issued the existing order for modification three years after the last support order without showing a substantial change of circumstances. Either may apply at any time based on a substantial change of circumstances. A modification may not take effect before notice of the modification petition is given to the respondent. Notice may be civil service or acceptance of a copy followed by timely filing and proof through certified-mail receipt.

## Structured propositions

- The issuing authority controls the modification path: the court for a court order, or DHHS when DHHS issued the legal order under RSA 161-C.
- Three years supplies a no-substantial-change route; a substantial change can support an earlier petition.
- Prompt service matters because the effective date cannot precede notice.
- The statute addresses reimbursement or adjustment for qualifying post-notice overpayments after modification.
