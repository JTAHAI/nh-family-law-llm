## Source-grounded extract (93 words)

The relocation section applies after a parenting or divorce petition and to a residence in which the child lives at least 150 days per year, subject to stated exceptions. A parent generally may not relocate the child without a court order. Sixty days' notice is presumed reasonable. The relocating parent first must prove by a preponderance that the move has a legitimate purpose and that the location is reasonable in light of that purpose; the burden then shifts to the other parent to prove the move is not in the child's best interest.
