## Source-grounded extract (82 words)

In a proceeding to establish or modify parenting time, except an RSA 173-B matter, the parents shall develop and file a parenting plan; if they cannot, the court may develop one. A plan may address decision-making and residential responsibility, information and electronic access, school residence, holidays and weekends, transportation and exchange, relocation, review and adjustment, and dispute resolution. Each plan must contain a detailed schedule specifying each parent's periods of residential responsibility or non-residential parenting time, without designating either parent as “primary.
