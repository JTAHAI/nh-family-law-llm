## Source-grounded extract (83 words)

A New Hampshire court with custody jurisdiction may decline it if this state is an inconvenient forum and another state is more appropriate. The court must permit party submissions and consider all relevant factors, including domestic violence and which state can best protect the family, the child's time outside New Hampshire, distance, relative finances, party agreement, location of evidence, each court's ability to decide promptly, and familiarity with the facts. The court may stay the custody matter while retaining the related divorce case.
