## Source-grounded extract (79 words)

Because children do best when both parents have stable and meaningful involvement, it is New Hampshire policy—unless shown not to be in a particular child's best interest—to encourage approximately equal parenting time if it is in the child's best interest, encourage shared rights and responsibilities after separation or divorce, encourage parents to develop their own plan unless abuse or neglect evidence exists, grant wide discretion in plan development, and consider both the statutory best-interest factors and safety.

## Structured propositions

- New Hampshire has an express policy favoring meaningful involvement and encouraging approximately equal time when that result serves the child’s best interests.
- The policy is not an irrebuttable entitlement to equal time; RSA 461-A:6 and safety remain controlling.
