## Source-grounded extract (53 words)

In determining best interests, including residential responsibility, the court shall not treat as an adverse factor a parent's raising a child consistent with the child's biological sex, including references to the child and mental-health or medical decisions based on biological sex. The paragraph does not limit consideration of other legally relevant best-interest factors.
