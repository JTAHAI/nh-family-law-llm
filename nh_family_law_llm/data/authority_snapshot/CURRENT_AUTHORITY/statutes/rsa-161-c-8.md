## Source-grounded extract (89 words)

If no legal support order exists, the department may serve the responsible parent with a notice of hearing and finding of financial responsibility and require the parent to appear and show why responsibility or amount is incorrect or should be rescinded or modified. After hearing, the commissioner may establish periodic support, accrued or accruing debt, or both. The hearing notice must be served at least 20 days before the hearing and state the amount claimed, debt or future periodic support, applicable public assistance, and the recipient and dependent children.

## Structured propositions

- DHHS can establish an administrative support obligation under RSA 161-C when no legal order exists, following statutory notice and hearing procedures.
- The served notice and resulting decision—not a payment ledger alone—identify an RSA 161-C administrative order.
