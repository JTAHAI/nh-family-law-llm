## Source-grounded extract (86 words)

In the first pleading or an attached sworn affidavit, each party must provide the child's present whereabouts, places lived during the last five years, and persons with whom the child lived, if reasonably ascertainable. The filing must identify prior custody participation, proceedings that could affect the case, and nonparties with physical custody or claimed rights. Each party has a continuing duty to report relevant proceedings. When disclosure would jeopardize health, safety, or liberty, identifying information must be sealed unless the court orders disclosure after a hearing.

## Structured propositions

- A complete five-year child-residence and proceeding history is a jurisdictional evidence requirement.
- Protective sealing is available for genuine safety risks, but it requires a sworn allegation and judicial process.
