## Source-grounded extract (102 words)

Adjusted gross income” means gross income, less court-ordered or administratively ordered support actually paid to others, fifty percent of actual self-employment tax paid, mandatory retirement contributions, actual state income taxes paid, and qualifying child-care or medical-support amounts paid by the obligor for the children in the order. An “approximately equal parenting schedule” is one where each parent has parenting time for greater than 40 percent of the annual schedule. A “substantially shared parenting schedule” is one where each parent has greater than 35 percent. “Substantially similar incomes” means the difference between the parents' gross monthly incomes is not greater than 10 percent.

## Structured propositions

- Gross income is broadly defined and includes earned and unearned income from listed and analogous sources.
- Ordinary rent, vehicle payments, food, utilities, and general household expenses are not listed automatic adjusted-gross-income deductions.
- The court may impute earning capacity when a parent voluntarily becomes unemployed or underemployed, subject to statutory limits.
- The 2025 definitions create numerical thresholds for approximately equal time, substantially shared time, and substantially similar incomes.

## Currentness and limits

2024 amendments affecting shared-parenting and income definitions became effective January 1, 2025.
