## Source-grounded extract (97 words)

A permanent parental-rights order may be modified when the parties agree; for repeated intentional and unwarranted interference if a change serves the child's best interests; when clear and convincing evidence shows the present environment is detrimental and the benefit outweighs harm from change; when substantially equal responsibility is not working and change serves the child; for a minimal or no-time-allocation change that serves the child; for materially changed travel or work circumstances; or, after at least five years, when the prior schedule was based on the child's young age. The moving party bears the burden of proof.
