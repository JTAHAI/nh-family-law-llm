## Source-grounded extract (82 words)

A “child-custody determination” is an order providing for legal custody, physical custody, or visitation and includes permanent, temporary, initial, and modification orders, but not child support or another monetary obligation. A “child-custody proceeding” includes divorce, separation, neglect, abuse, dependency, guardianship, paternity, parental-rights, termination, and domestic-violence matters in which custody or visitation is at issue. “Home state” generally means the state where the child lived with a parent or person acting as a parent for at least six consecutive months immediately before commencement.

## Structured propositions

- UCCJEA jurisdiction is analytically separate from monetary child-support jurisdiction.
- Temporary absences count toward the six-month home-state period.
- The statute’s definitions apply across several family, juvenile, guardianship, and protective-order contexts.
