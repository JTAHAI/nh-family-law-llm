## Source-grounded extract (65 words)

When the department is subrogated to a legal support order, the commissioner may issue a notice of support debt. The notice must state the accrued or accruing debt, the order terms and children, available collection remedies, a demand for payment within 20 days, application of collection proceeds, and contest procedures. Collection generally may begin after 20 days from service, subject to the statute's jeopardy provisions.

## Structured propositions

- This section addresses administrative collection of debt under an existing legal order; it does not itself recalculate the underlying court-ordered monthly amount.
- A recipient should distinguish a debt/collection notice from the original Uniform Support Order or administrative support decision.
