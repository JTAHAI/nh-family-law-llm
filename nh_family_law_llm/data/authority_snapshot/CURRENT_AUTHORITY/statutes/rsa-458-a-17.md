## Source-grounded extract (77 words)

Except for emergency jurisdiction, New Hampshire may not exercise jurisdiction when, at commencement, a custody proceeding is already pending in another state exercising jurisdiction substantially in conformity with the UCCJEA, unless that proceeding ends or is stayed because New Hampshire is the more convenient forum. The New Hampshire court must review party-supplied information, stay its proceeding, and communicate with the other court. In a modification case, it must also determine whether an enforcement proceeding is pending elsewhere.

## Structured propositions

- Parallel proceedings require prompt disclosure and court-to-court communication.
- The court may stay, enjoin, or condition a modification proceeding when interstate enforcement is already underway.
