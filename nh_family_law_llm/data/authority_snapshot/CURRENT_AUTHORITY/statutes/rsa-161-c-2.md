## Source-grounded extract (65 words)

Legal order of support” means any judgment or order, including an order in a final divorce decree for dependent-child support, issued by a New Hampshire court, a court of another state, or any body authorized by law to issue and modify support orders. “Department” means the New Hampshire Department of Health and Human Services, and “obligor” means the person found legally liable for child support.

## Structured propositions

- RSA 161-C recognizes both judicial and legally authorized administrative support orders.
- The nature of the signed order—not merely the fact that payments pass through DHHS—determines the proper modification forum.
