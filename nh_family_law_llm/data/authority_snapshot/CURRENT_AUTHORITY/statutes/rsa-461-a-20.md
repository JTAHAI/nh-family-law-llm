## Source-grounded extract (45 words)

A statutory reference to “custody” of minor children means the allocation of parental rights and responsibilities under RSA 461-A. A “custodial parent” means a parent with 50 percent or more of residential responsibility, and a “non-custodial parent” means a parent with less than 50 percent.

## Structured propositions

- The application should translate older or external custody terminology into New Hampshire’s parental-rights framework.
- A parent with exactly 50 percent residential responsibility falls within the statutory custodial-parent definition for cross-references.
