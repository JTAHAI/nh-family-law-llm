# NH child-support review

1. Obtain the signed Uniform Support Order or served RSA 161-C administrative decision; collection or withholding alone does not identify the issuer.
2. Determine the RSA 458-C:7 route: at least three years since the last order, or a supported substantial change before three years.
3. Rebuild the calculation inputs from current income evidence and the current official guideline table/worksheet. Do not reuse an old table without checking its date.
4. Audit only statutory adjusted-gross-income deductions and separately analyze RSA 458-C:5 deviation factors.
5. For parenting-schedule deviations, verify ordered annual percentages, income similarity, all specified 50/50 cost-sharing conditions, extraordinary circumstances, and best interests.
6. Treat parenting modification and support modification as separate requested relief. Do not self-adjust support.
7. Preserve proof of statutory notice because modification is prospective under the governing rule.
