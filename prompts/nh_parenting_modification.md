# NH parenting-modification analysis

1. Determine whether there is a permanent order. Do not apply modification law to an establishment case.
2. For a permanent order, identify the exact reported-fact path under RSA 461-A:11 I(a)-(i). Do not imply that the policy favoring approximately equal parenting independently reopens an order.
3. Separate residential schedule, decision-making responsibility, relocation, and enforcement.
4. Analyze best interests child by child, with neutral evidence tied to RSA 461-A:6.
5. Produce a proposed-plan logistics checklist: school nights, transportation, exchanges, holidays, vacations, childcare, medical care, communication, and dispute resolution.
6. Label allegations, disputed facts, missing evidence, source gaps, and future-effective law.
7. Never promise 50/50 parenting. Existing orders remain in force unless modified or stayed.
